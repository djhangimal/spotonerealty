What I do

I work with residential, commercial and investment real estate in Panama. Before moving a property or making a decision, I review price, location and market timing.

01

Reading

Price, location, competition and timing shape the decision. Listing or searching is not enough; it has to make sense within the market.

02

Focus

I define the structure of the operation: what is actually moving, for whom and under which conditions.

03

Execution

Exposure, follow-up and negotiation, without improvising.

04

Integration

When the operation requires it, I coordinate key parts of arrival, stay and local logistics: legal structure, account opening, appointments and movement during the visit.

Who I work with

Owners and landlords I work with owners and landlords who need to organize how to enter the market: price, presentation and conditions.
Buyers and tenants I work with buyers and tenants who want to understand what they are committing to before deciding.
Investors I review location, price and liquidity. I only move forward when the risk–return relationship is clear.
International clients I support international clients who need to understand how the Panama market actually works.

If you need to review a real estate decision in Panama, we can look at it together.
